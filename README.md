# Nakuru, Seen Through Heat

### Mapping Nakuru's Urban Heat Landscape with Landsat, 2010–2026

Nakuru is a city shaped by its natural environment, agriculture, commerce and a rapidly changing urban landscape.

**Nakuru, Seen Through Heat** explores this changing landscape through satellite imagery, looking at Land Surface Temperature, vegetation, built-up surfaces and seasonal patterns between 2010 and 2026.

## What does the project explore?

* How Nakuru's landscape has changed over time
* Where surface temperatures are highest
* How vegetation varies between seasons
* The relationship between built-up areas and surface temperature
* Dry-season and wet-season thermal patterns
* Urban Heat Island patterns across Nakuru

## Data & Methods

The project uses Landsat satellite imagery processed in Google Earth Engine.

The analysis covers:

**2010 · 2014 · 2018 · 2021 · 2022 · 2024 · 2026**

Key indicators include:

* **LST** - Land Surface Temperature
* **NDVI** - vegetation
* **NDBI** - built-up surfaces
* **NDWI** - surface water

The analysis uses seasonal composites to compare Nakuru's landscape and thermal conditions through time.
